CircuitBridge Learning is a fictional training business created for the Agentic Systems coursework by Habib Shouman. Its mission is to make practical AI and electronics education approachable through small, guided workshops for adult learners. Its audience includes adult beginners, university students, and small teams seeking applied technical skills.

The business offers AI Foundations, Python Launchpad, and Edge AI Lab. The first two workshops welcome beginners; Edge AI Lab is intended for learners with basic Python and electronics knowledge. Its value proposition combines small groups, plain-language explanations, tangible projects, and transparent published tuition and prerequisites. The companion PDF contains the detailed catalog, prices, delivery modes, and operating policies.

The fictional team consists of Leila Farah, curriculum lead; Karim Nassar, electronics mentor; and Mira Haddad, learner-support coordinator. The assistant can answer documented business questions, collect authorized follow-up requests, and log feedback or unanswered questions. A follow-up record is an expression of interest, not a booking. Dates, available seats, and tax treatment must not be invented. This business has no real customers, credentials, or operating address.
