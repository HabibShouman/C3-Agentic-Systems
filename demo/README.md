# C3 demonstration

Habib Shouman

Open C3_demonstration.webm in a current browser or video player. This is a silent automated recording of the actual Gradio interface using genuine Gemini responses and fictional contact details. It shows general advice, missing lead fields, completed lead recording, feedback, an unknown question, refusal to log, and a separate viewer of the actual source/records/reflection events.

The evidence viewer is labeled separately from the customer interface. Responses were not mocked, edited, or injected. The recording does not establish general reliability. Browser/version/times, records, and trace files are in evidence/ui_verification.json and evidence/ui_demo/. Screenshots were used only for internal QA.


The delivered recording is a compact VP8 derivative at three frames per second. Its timeline is 155.333 seconds; the original is 154.96 seconds, with final-frame padding from frame-rate conversion. No sequence cuts, speed changes, or response replacement were made. Original and derivative hashes, decode checks, and timing are recorded in evidence/video_transcode_verification.json. The original file is preserved privately.
